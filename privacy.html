Daylit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026
Data Not Collected

Daylit is a private, on-device journaling app. Your journal belongs to you, and only you. This policy explains — in plain language — exactly how Daylit handles your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Daylit has no account system, no servers, and makes no network requests. Your entries, photos, mood analysis, and settings are stored only on your device. We have no way to see, access, or receive any of your data.

On-device analysis

Mood (sentiment) scoring and keyword extraction are performed entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device NaturalLanguage framework. No text, photo, or analysis result is ever uploaded or transmitted.

Optional features you control

  • Apple Health (State of Mind). If you turn this on, Daylit writes your daily mood to Apple Health on your device. Daylit does not read any health data. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings › Health.
  • iCloud sync. If you turn this on, your entries sync through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. This data goes to your personal iCloud — never to Daylit or any third-party server. It is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Notifications. An optional daily reminder is scheduled locally on your device.
  • Photos. If you attach a photo, it is read with your permission and stored only inside the app on your device.

Purchases

The optional "AI Insights" unlock is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Daylit never sees your payment details. Purchases are subject to Apple's terms.

Analytics & advertising

Daylit contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Your control

You can export all of your data as Markdown or JSON at any time from Settings. Deleting the app permanently removes all of your data from your device. An optional passcode and Face ID keep your journal private.

Children

Daylit does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Daylit is a single, offline app. The strongest privacy guarantee is architectural: there is no server to leak, because there is no server at all.
