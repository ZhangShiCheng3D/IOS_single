FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. Daylit has no account and no servers. If you turn on iCloud Sync, entries sync through your own private iCloud — never our servers.

How do I unlock AI Insights?

Open the Trends tab and tap “Unlock Insights”, or go to Settings → AI Insights. It’s a one-time purchase — no subscription. Already bought it? Tap “Restore Purchase”.

How do I back up or move my journal?

Settings → Export lets you save everything as Markdown or JSON at any time. You can also enable optional iCloud Sync to keep devices in sync.

How do I save my mood to Apple Health?

Settings → Sync & Health → “Save mood to Apple Health” (iOS 18 and later). Daylit only writes your mood; it never reads health data.

How do I set a daily reminder?

Settings → Reminders → turn on “Daily reminder” and pick a time.

How do I lock the app?

Settings → Privacy → App Lock (Face ID or passcode).
